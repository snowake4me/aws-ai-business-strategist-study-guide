# Exam Traps

Seeded from the 2026-09-26 Tutorials Dojo attempt. Unlike AIP-C01, where this file was
deliberately thin because nothing fooled the candidate, here **four of six misses were
the same trap**, so it opens with one live entry and a drill.

---

## Trap #1 — Register over stem facts

The stem states a fact. One option honors it; another option contradicts it but sounds
like the answer the exam usually wants (prudent, people-centric, strategic, or simply
reuses the stem's words). The sound wins.

| Register that won | Fact it ignored | Q |
|---|---|---|
| Prudent: "run a short pilot" | Stated volume and peak; process already in production | 9 |
| People-centric: "alerts reviewed by technicians" | Required dimension was *efficiency* | 10 |
| AI-forward: "learn from historical records" | Policy fully stated as three rules | 13 |
| Stem's own words: "recent sales data" | Retraining happens every two months | 18 |

Three sub-shapes worth recognizing on sight:

- **Scope shrink.** A smaller, safer-sounding version of the right action ("small
  set", "typical", "sample", "subset") when the stem gave a volume profile. Q9.
- **Adjacent substitution.** A metric or action that shares a noun with the
  requirement but not the meaning. "Reviewed by technicians" for *technician
  efficiency*. Q10.
- **Bait after the answer.** The stem states the decisive fact early, then closes with
  an impressive-sounding fact (volume, years of data, scale) that points the other
  way. The late fact is bait. Q13.

### A lead on when it happens

All four of these show 0:00 on TD's per-question timer. Nothing was skipped, so the
zeros are an artifact, probably from pausing the exam mid-question. If the pauses did
land on these four, the trap fired on coming back cold. Unconfirmed. Test it next
attempt by noting any interruption.

### The drill

1. **Name the fact in the stem that eliminates each option you reject.** If no fact
   can be named, the choice is being made on tone. On Select-TWO, name the fact that
   *supports* each element you accept as well.
2. After any pause or interruption, **re-read the stem from the top** before looking
   at the options again.

Costs about 20 s per question against a 120 s budget.

## Quick-recall cheat card

1. Find the fact. Every stem plants one. Volume, peak, cadence, a stated policy, a
   required dimension.
2. Kill options with it before judging tone.
3. "Small", "typical", "sample" against a stated volume: dead.
4. Shares a noun with the requirement, not the meaning: dead.
5. Impressive fact placed *after* the decisive one: bait.
6. Select-TWO: justify each accepted element, not just the rejected ones.
