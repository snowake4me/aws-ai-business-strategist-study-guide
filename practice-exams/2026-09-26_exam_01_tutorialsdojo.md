### Sample-size caveat

D2 is 14 questions, so one question is 7.1 points. "Down in the 70s" is three
questions. The domain-level signal is *which* two domains leak, not the exact rate.
Task-level splits from a 65-question form would be 1–3 questions each; do not read
them.
